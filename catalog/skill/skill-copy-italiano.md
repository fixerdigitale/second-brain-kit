# Persuasive copy, but in Italian and within the rules

**The problem.** The copywriting skills I find (Hormozi, Cialdini, Kennedy, Ogilvy) are American. Used as they are, they produce texts that read as translated, emphatic, with fake scarcity ("only 3 spots left") that in Italy can count as unfair commercial practices.

**How I solved it.** I install the method skills from their sources and put in front of them copy-italia, a short skill I wrote. It says what to change for Italy: concrete B2B tone, urgency and scarcity only if real (Italian Consumer Code and Legislative Decree 145/2007), guarantees only if the seller can keep them, no inflated values. At the top of each text it writes what it adapted. The last pass is italiano-scrittura-anti-ai, which removes calques and AI-text tics.

**Why this way.** The American method works for me, the tone doesn't: the Italian audience of professionals and small businesses distrusts emphasis. The legal filter keeps me from writing a promise the client can't keep.

**When you need it.** If you write, or have someone write, landing pages, offers or sales emails in Italian. The same idea works for any market: a short skill with your country's tone and rules in front of the American method skills.

**When you DON'T need it.** If the client already gives you the copy: it should be laid out as it is, not rewritten. If you only write technical or internal texts, italiano-scrittura-anti-ai is enough.
