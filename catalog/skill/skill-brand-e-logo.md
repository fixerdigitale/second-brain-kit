# Brand, voice and logo with a method

**The problem.** For a new brand, or a client without a clear identity, the AI jumped straight to colors and logo without having pinned down who the brand is, who it speaks to and how.

**How I solved it.** I use third-party skills in order. brand-context saves identity, audience and positioning in a file the others read. Then brand-strategy, brand-voice, brand-identity, brand-naming and brand-guidelines. Then logo-design (brief, concepts, clean SVGs, test at 16 px and in one color) and brandkit for the presentation boards.

**Why this way.** The context file read by all the skills avoids re-explaining the brand at every step. Testing the logo at 16 px and in one color immediately discards ideas that don't hold up as an icon.

**When you need it.** New brands, rebrandings, clients who arrive without guidelines.

**When you DON'T need it.** If the brand already has a settled manual: use that. For an important brand the AI prepares the work, but I leave the final choice to a designer.

**How to get it.** Third-party skills, from the source: https://github.com/arnabbagxd/Brand-building-skills (brand-*), https://github.com/kaankiziltug/logo-design-skill, https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill (brandkit; license to be verified).
