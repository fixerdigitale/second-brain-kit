# Triage: what I do first when everything is urgent

**The problem.** When client requests, bugs and "urgent" messages arrive together, choosing the order costs me more than the work itself.

**How I solved it.** I use a third-party skill that sorts things into P0-P3 by the cost of waiting. It says what to do right away, what waits, and when it's worth interrupting the work in progress. It learns my real priority rules from my corrections.

**Why this way.** The "how much does waiting cost" criterion is more honest than "who shouts loudest". I keep it for pile-up moments, not as a fixed system.

**When you need it.** Packed weeks, coming back after days away, many channels requests arrive from.

**When you DON'T need it.** If you have few requests at a time, or if a pipeline or your board already say the order. It doesn't estimate times and doesn't make roadmaps.

**How to get it.** Third-party skill from the ClawHub registry: https://clawhub.ai/ivangdavila/skills/triage (author's page: https://clawic.com/skills/triage). It's an open registry: read it all before installing.
