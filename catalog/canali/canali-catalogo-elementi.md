# A catalog of everything I produce

**The problem.** With AI I produce a lot: reports, slides, images, maps, videos. After two weeks I couldn't find anything, and I no longer knew what had gone to the client and what was a draft of mine.

**How I solved it.** Every item goes into a catalog with project, task and use: internal or external. The commands that publish reports and proposals register them by themselves; for everything else there's a dedicated command. The client area shows only items marked as external. If it isn't clear what something is for or who it's for, I'm asked before it gets created. The package has the vault version: an `ELEMENTI.md` file with a table and a rule for CLAUDE.md that has Claude keep it updated.

**Why this way.** Finding costs less than redoing. And the internal/external column stops a draft with private notes from ending up in front of a client.

**When you need it.** If you produce more than a few documents a week or work for several clients.

**When you DON'T need it.** If you produce little and per-client folders are enough to find everything: an extra index is just one more file to maintain.

**How to get it.** Copy `ELEMENTI.md` into the root of the vault and paste `regola-per-CLAUDE.md` into the vault's CLAUDE.md. Step-by-step instructions: [package guide](../../pacchetti/canali-catalogo-elementi/LEGGIMI.md).
